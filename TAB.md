# Still Water

*for solo guitar — standard tuning (E A D G B e)*

**Tempo:** slow 6/8, about 50–60 dotted-quarter beats per minute. Slower is better than faster.
**Feel:** let everything ring. Never damp a string unless you have to.

---

## The four shapes

The top two strings (B and high e) stay **open** the whole piece. That's the trick — they hum underneath everything like a held breath, and the bass moves around beneath them.

```
   Em9      Cmaj7      G6      Asus2
e|--0--    e|--0--    e|--0--   e|--0--
B|--0--    B|--0--    B|--0--   B|--0--
G|--0--    G|--0--    G|--0--   G|--2--
D|--4--    D|--2--    D|--0--   D|--2--
A|--2--    A|--3--    A|--x--   A|--0--
E|--0--    E|--x--    E|--3--   E|--x--
```

Two fingers each. Nothing to grip hard.

---

## Picking hand

One pattern, six notes, repeated forever:

**thumb — thumb — index — middle — ring — middle**

on strings: **bass — 4 — 3 — 2 — 1 — 2**

Say it out loud a few times before you play it. Once your hand knows it, you can stop reading.

## Section B — play twice

Same shapes, same left hand. The only change: the last two notes of each bar are a small melody on the high E string instead of the usual ring-then-middle.

```
e|-----------------3---2---|-----------------0---3---|-----------------2---0---|-----------------0-------|
B|-------------0-----------|-------------0-----------|-------------0-----------|-------------0-----------|
G|---------0---------------|---------0---------------|---------0---------------|---------2---------------|
D|-----4-------------------|-----2-------------------|-----0-------------------|-----2-------------------|
A|-------------------------|-3-----------------------|-------------------------|-0-----------------------|
E|-0-----------------------|-------------------------|-3-----------------------|-------------------------|
   Em9                       Cmaj7                     G6                        Asus2
```

The melody line is just: **G – F♯ | E – G | F♯ – E | E**. Hum it if you want.

## Return to Section A, then end

```
e|-----------------0-------|-----------------------<12>-------|
B|-------------0-----------|------------<12>------------------|
G|---------0---------------|-<12>-----------------------------|
D|-----4-------------------|----------------------------------|
A|-------------------------|----------------------------------|
E|-0-----------------------|----------------------------------|
   Em9 — let ring            natural harmonics, 12th fret
```

For the harmonics: rest a fingertip *lightly* directly over the 12th fret wire — don't press — pluck, and lift off. Bell tones. Let them fade all the way out before you put the guitar down.

---

## Structure

**A – A – B – B – A – ending.** About three minutes at the right tempo. Loop it as long as you like; it's built to go around.

---

## If you're too tired to read tab

Just thumb-strum the four shapes, six slow beats each, in a loop:

> **Em9 → Cmaj7 → G6 → Asus2**

That alone will do most of the work. The piece is really only an ornament on those four chords.

---

## A few notes on playing it tired

- **Pair it with your breath.** Breathe in across one bar, out across the next. Four bars = two full breaths. At this tempo that lands you around five or six breaths a minute, which is roughly where your nervous system starts to settle on its own. You don't have to try to relax — the tempo does it.
- **Play it too quietly.** Quieter than feels right. Anxiety tends to show up in the picking hand as force; playing at a whisper makes it obvious and gives you something concrete to soften.
- **Let mistakes go past.** Everything here is E minor and adjacent — a wrong note is almost always still in the key. Keep the pattern going and it'll sound intentional.
- **Drop the pinky if the stretch bugs you.** The 4th fret on the D string in Em9 is the only slightly awkward reach. Play it open (D instead of F♯) and it becomes Em7. Just as pretty, zero effort.
